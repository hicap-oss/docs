An **Organization** is the account where a company or team manages shared Hicap resources. **Users** belong to Organizations and have access according to their permissions. Users can also manage personal keys in their account settings.

## Organizations

An **Organization** groups shared users and Applications and provides the account context for their usage and billing. A User can belong to more than one Organization. Select the Organization in which you want to work when managing its resources or reviewing its usage.

## Users

A **User** is an individual person. Within an Organization, a User can work with the resources and usage information their permissions allow. A User's personal keys are managed separately in their account settings.

## Who owns what

Ownership determines which resources are shared with an Organization and which are managed personally.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Organization-owned" icon="building">
    Shared Applications and their Connections belong to the **Organization**. Users with the appropriate access can manage them and review their usage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="User-owned (personal)" icon="user">
    A User can manage personal keys for tooling such as an IDE assistant, local script, or API client. These keys appear in the User's account settings, separate from the Organization's shared Applications.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For personal-key usage and billing, check the available reporting and account context in your deployment. Do not assume that personal aggregate reporting is available or that it uses the same view as shared Organization usage.

## Access

Organization access depends on the User's role and the resource. Check your Organization's current permissions before granting access or managing shared credentials. Users manage their own personal keys in account settings.

## How this connects to attribution

Organizations and Users describe *who* people are and *what they can reach* — this is different from how a request is labeled. For the structural hierarchy that spend rolls up through, see [Applications, Connections & Keys](/concepts/applications-connections-keys). For the labels you choose per request, see [Tags, Dimensions & Segments](/concepts/tags-dimensions-segments).
