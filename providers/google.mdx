## Output formats

The standard Gemini 2.5 Flash and Flash-Lite models generate text; image/audio generation requires a suitable specialized model and endpoint. See [Google’s Flash model reference](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models/gemini-2.5-flash) and [Flash-Lite reference](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models/gemini-2.5-flash-lite). Native capability is separate from Hicap endpoint support.
