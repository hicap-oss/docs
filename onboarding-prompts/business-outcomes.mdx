Use this prompt after configuring the Hicap client and attribution. It helps you measure actual workflow results using your application's analytics or event system. Attribution describes requests; outcome tracking establishes what happened in the business workflow.

## Full prompt

```text wrap title="Business outcomes"
Help me connect this application's AI usage to measurable business outcomes.

1. Read repository instructions and inspect existing Hicap integration,
   attribution, business workflows, analytics, and event tracking. Reuse the
   existing client and tags; preserve routing, models, and provider settings.
   Identify missing prerequisites and inaccessible or unresolved paths.

2. Propose measurable outcomes grounded in actual workflows, such as a resolved
   support case or a completed document review. Document each outcome's
   definition, source of truth, completion event, reporting window, and
   relationship to AI usage. Reuse approved definitions; ask me to resolve
   ambiguous business definitions before implementing tracking.
   A successful model response or feature tag is not proof of a business outcome.

3. Implement tracking using the existing analytics or event system. Record
   actual outcomes at the owning workflow boundary and correlate them with the
   relevant workflow and AI usage using appropriate internal identifiers.
   Keep secrets and personal data out of attribution tags and reports. Preserve
   existing consent, access, and retention rules. Do not invent Hicap outcome
   APIs, new product capabilities, or unsupported attribution fields.

4. Define cost per completed outcome for the same workflow scope and time
   window. State how retries, failed attempts, multiple model calls, duplicate
   events, and delayed outcomes are handled. Do not divide by zero; report
   missing costs or outcomes as unavailable rather than zero. If reliable
   correlation or cost data is unavailable, report the gap instead of claiming
   the metric is implemented. Do not claim causal business impact from correlation.

5. Test actual application paths with mocked workflows and provider responses.
   Check successful completion, failure, retries, duplicate delivery, and
   correlation across asynchronous work where relevant. Separate mock-tested
   tracking from deployed behavior and fresh observed outcomes.
   Do not deploy or generate paid traffic without approval.

Summarize changes, outcome definitions, data sources, checks, gaps, and the
deployment and fresh-traffic verification still required. Preserve earlier
reports and follow existing repository conventions.
```

Review the resulting changes before deploying. Start with [client setup](/onboarding-prompts/configure-client) and [attribution](/onboarding-prompts/attribution) if those prerequisites are missing. Return to the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
