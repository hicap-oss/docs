### General

Everything you need to know about using HiCap on a day-to-day basis

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What exactly does HiCAP do in plain terms?">
    HiCAP gives you fast, secure access to top closed AI models—like GPT-4.1 and Sonnet 4 —at a fraction of the usual cost. We bulk-reserve compute from leading cloud providers and pass the savings to you, all through a simple API.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is “reserved throughput” different from pay-as-you-go tokens?">
    Reserved throughput locks in your compute and pricing—no rate limits, no surprise overages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much can customers typically save, and what drives those savings?">
    Typical savings are up to 25%. Your savings depend on your model usage blend, commitment, and capacity needs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which models and clouds do you cover today?">
    We support all major models across OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and all top open source models. All across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud and top Neo Clouds. More models and clouds are coming soon, let us know what you need!
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a minimum commitment or lock-in period?">
    No long-term lock-in. Start monthly, scale up or down anytime. Want a discount? We offer extra savings for longer commitments.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if we burst above our reserved capacity?">
    No stress. You can burst above your reservation as needed—just contact us, and we'll help you scale up instantly. Your service stays smooth, even during traffic surges.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can we mix HiCAP reservations with our existing provider credits?">
    Yes! You can use HiCAP for part of your workload and keep using existing provider credits elsewhere. Many teams blend both for maximum flexibility and savings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Security

Your trust and data security are our top priority

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is my data safe with HiCap?">
    Yes. We use bank-grade encryption (AES-256) and secure communication protocols (TLS 1.3) to protect all user data.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does HiCap share my personal information with third parties?">
    No. We never sell or share your data with third parties without your explicit consent. Your information is only used to provide our services.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if there's a security breach?">
    HiCap follows strict incident-response protocols. If a breach were ever to occur, users would be notified immediately and supported with protective measures.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I delete my data from HiCap?">
    Absolutely. You can request data deletion at any time, and we will securely remove your information from our systems in compliance with privacy regulations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is HiCap compliant with industry security standards?">
    Yes. HiCap follows best practices such as encryption, role-based access control, and can be configured to meet compliance requirements like SOC 2 or GDPR.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I need to connect HiCap to external APIs?">
    You decide. HiCap gives you full control over which applications go out, while keeping internal data and processing private.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does HiCap protect sensitive information?">
    By keeping requests inside your infrastructure, HiCap eliminates exposure to public endpoints. Combined with encryption in transit and at rest, your data stays safe end-to-end.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can HiCap be deployed inside my AWS account?">
    Yes. HiCap can be installed within your AWS environment, ensuring that all processing and data requests remain private and isolated.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
